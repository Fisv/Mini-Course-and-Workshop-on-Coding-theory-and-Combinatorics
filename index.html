Workshop objectives

The mini-course introduces Vietnamese students and early-career researchers to coding theory and combinatorics, with applications in communication, data storage, and cryptography. The workshop brings Vietnamese and international researchers together to share recent results and start new collaborations.

Venues

1–3 and 5 December
VIASM, 161 Huynh Thuc Khang Street, Hanoi

4 December
VinUniversity, Vinhomes Ocean Park, Gia Lam, Hanoi

Days 1–3 · Mini-course

The first three days are a mini-course for students and early-career researchers at the Vietnam Institute for Advanced Study in Mathematics (VIASM). Lectures start from Hamming distance and linear codes and build up to permutation codes, constrained coding and codes for DNA-based storage.

Days 4–5 · Workshop and conference

On 4 December the workshop moves to VinUniversity for a morning of open discussion and an afternoon excursion. On 5 December the conference at VIASM brings two keynotes and ten invited talks. The event honours Professor Yeow Meng Chee and Professor Tuvi Etzion for their contributions to the field.
